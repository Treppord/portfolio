02 — About

Curious about how things work, one layer down

I wrote my first code at 13, in Java, to make mods for Minecraft. That tinkering turned into mods published on CurseForge, and into a habit of wanting to understand what happens underneath.

These days I work across the stack: Fabric mods and modding APIs in Java, game engines built from scratch in C, Rust and Go, and websites, apps and APIs in TypeScript and Go. My Frontend Design & Development studies at the Hogeschool van Amsterdam made design matter to me as much as the code.
